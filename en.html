Trip plan · detox by bike · northern edition

Northbound: the Wadden Islands, Friesland
and Groningen, from Shabbat to Shabbat

Sunday August 9 → Tuesday September 8, 2026. A loop out of Amsterdam entirely within the Netherlands, heading north with the southwest wind at your back: dunes, two Wadden Islands reached purely by boat, Friesland, the sea you can walk across at low tide, 5.000-year-old dolmens and the canals of Giethoorn. A rented bike, remote work in the morning, riding in the afternoon, and the four Shabbatot locked in. You get back with two buffer days before Rosh Hashanah (candle lighting: Friday Sep 11).

Step 3

Where to sleep

  • Vrienden op de Fiets — the Dutch network of rooms in family homes for cyclists: €27,50 per night with breakfast. Membership €10/year for residents abroad, online access to the map. The north is full of addresses (Alkmaar, Texel, Friesland, Groningen, Drenthe) — this is YOUR network for the nights on the road.
  • Hotels/apartments at the anchor stops — Amsterdam (9 nights at the start near Kastelenstraat/Zuid — the kosher hub — and 4 at the end), Leeuwarden, Groningen. Book the nights of the 4 Shabbatot and the 2 island nights now: August on Texel/Vlieland sells out fast.

Three key facts

① There is no Chabad north of Lelystad — the north runs on the local NIK communities. Useful fact: the three northern communities (Groningen, Leeuwarden, Zwolle) share the same itinerant NIK rabbi, Shmuel Spiero — a single contact can unlock both weekends. ② HaCarmel (Amsterdam's iconic restaurant) has been closed since 2022 even though 2026 guides still list it. ③ On Fridays, kosher Amsterdam closes 13:30–16:00: Shabbat shopping happens early.

Regular supermarket + kosher list

The Dutch rabbinate (NIK) publishes the Kasjroetlijst (kasjroet.nik.nl + its own app + the "Is it Kosher" app): it covers Albert Heijn, Jumbo, Dirk and Ekoplaza, including AH's own-brand products. With that, the entire north is solved at any supermarket: staples, fish, dairy with a hechsher, verified bread. NIG Groningen's own kashrut page points to that very list.

Fine print

The logistics, in detail

The three ferries (the backbone of week 2)

LegBoatPrice with bikeNote
Den Helder → TexelTESO, hourly, 20 min€ 6 returnNo reservation, first come first served

Vlieland → HarlingenDoeksen regular service, 1 h 30, 3×/day≈ € 22 + € 12–16Bike reservation mandatory in practice; the fast ferry does NOT take bikes

Plan B if the wind cancels the small boat: head back to Den Helder with TESO and cross the Afsluitdijk — the new fietspad (cycle path, 24 km) is open, but the locks at both ends remain under construction until 2027: you skip them with the free fietsbus (hourly, last departure 18:45, ~15 bikes, first come first served).

Trains with a bike (NS)

  • Fietskaart Dal (off-peak bike ticket) €7,95, all day, the whole network. August: no time restrictions. From Sep 1 the Mon–Fri ban applies 6:30–9:00 and 16:00–18:30 — your Friday Sep 4 train (~10:00) fits squarely in the valid window. The bike boards only through the doors with the symbol.

Wind, weather and light

  • Prevailing wind from the southwest (KNMI data): a tailwind heading north — that's why the loop is ridden in this direction and the return is by train.
  • August: highs 22–23 °C, lows 13 °C, ~11 days with some rain → a rain jacket is non-negotiable; on the coast and the islands the wind calls the shots.
  • Light (a verified fun fact): in August the north barely gains any evening light — sunset depends more on longitude than latitude at this time of year; Amsterdam (further west) has sunset 4-5 min later than Groningen. On Aug 15 the sun sets at 21:04 in Groningen; on Aug 28, at 20:35.

Remote work

  • Libraries: free entry and free wifi — the Forum Groningen is one of the best free offices in Europe; Amsterdam's OBA uses Publicroam (set it up once).
  • Free wifi on almost every NS Intercity. An EU data eSIM as your main connection (buy before leaving).

Apps to download before you go

  • Knooppuntnet or Fietsknoop (node-network route planner — Texel included: 57 nodes of its own) · Kasjroetlijst NIK / "Is it Kosher" · Hebcal (candle lighting per city) · the NS app · Buienradar (the national rain radar, a local religion) · bookings: teso.nl, waddenveer.nl, rederij-doeksen.nl, wadlopen.com.
